# Nutree Web Funnel — Psychology and Copy Standard

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** Final  
**Purpose:** Define the ethical psychological-investment model and the copy system for the fixed funnel  
**Experimentation:** None

---

## 1. Psychological objective

The user should reach payment with three honest beliefs:

1. **“Nutree understands my situation.”**
2. **“I helped build this plan.”**
3. **“Paying activates a useful system, not merely a number.”**

Psychological investment is not created by making the user wait or answer irrelevant questions. It is created when meaningful effort produces visible, personally relevant output.

The funnel should therefore alternate between:

```text
Input → acknowledgment → more precise input → personalized reward
```

The user must never feel that Nutree is collecting information without using it.

---

## 2. Ethical position

Nutree may use behavioral design to improve attention, comprehension, follow-through, and plan ownership.

Nutree must not use behavioral design to:

- Hide price.
- Hide renewal.
- Create false urgency.
- Exploit body insecurity.
- Make unsupported health predictions.
- Prevent editing.
- Make cancellation confusing.
- Force inaccurate defaults.
- Fabricate social proof.
- Pretend that a generic screen is personalized.
- Shame the user for abandoning or declining.

The funnel is successful when users knowingly choose a product they understand and then activate it—not when confused users complete a payment.

## 3. Core psychological mechanisms

### 3.1 Self-reference

People pay more attention to information connected to their own identity and goals.

Use:

- First name at major reflection points.
- The selected goal.
- The selected challenge.
- The selected desired outcome.
- The selected support style.

Do not place the name in every heading. Overuse feels generated and manipulative.

Recommended name placements:

- Reflection.
- Plan summary.
- Result headline.
- Paywall headline.
- Success/app claim.

### 3.2 Commitment and consistency

The user makes a sequence of small, truthful commitments:

- “This is what I want.”
- “This is what has stopped me.”
- “This is why it matters.”
- “These are my real body measurements.”
- “This is how I live.”
- “This is the kind of support I prefer.”

The final purchase is framed as activating the plan that matches those commitments.

Do not ask performative questions such as:

> “How committed are you to changing your life?”

That encourages socially desirable answers and can create shame. Ask about preferences and reality instead.

### 3.3 Effort justification

Meaningful effort can increase perceived value when the result clearly reflects that effort.

Requirements:

- Every answer changes calculation, reflection, example, or offer framing.
- The user sees intermediate summaries.
- The final result explains which inputs were used.
- The app preserves all completed work.

Effort becomes harmful when the user must repeat it or cannot see its effect.

### 3.4 Endowed progress

The funnel uses four named chapters and visible completion.

Good:

> Mục tiêu của bạn — hoàn thành  
> Cơ thể của bạn — đang thực hiện

Bad:

> 68% cá nhân hóa

Do not invent a personalization percentage. Show real completed sections.

### 3.5 Goal-gradient effect

Motivation rises as the user approaches a meaningful finish.

Use:

- Clear chapter boundaries.
- A short plan-summary screen before calculation.
- A result that appears promptly.
- A paywall framed as the final activation step.

Do not manipulate the progress bar by making early steps appear to count more or adding surprise steps after “almost done.”

### 3.6 Perceived listening

The reflection screen must transform answers into a coherent, human-sounding summary.

The purpose is not praise. It is proof of comprehension.

Good:

> Bạn muốn giảm mỡ để cảm thấy tự tin hơn, nhưng lịch trình bận khiến việc theo dõi dễ bị gián đoạn. Kế hoạch của bạn sẽ ưu tiên cách ghi nhận nhanh và những lựa chọn bữa ăn dễ duy trì.

Bad:

> Tuyệt vời! Bạn thật tuyệt vời và đã sẵn sàng thay đổi cuộc đời!

Avoid generic encouragement that could appear after any answer.

### 3.7 Ownership

Use language that reflects creation:

- “Kế hoạch bạn vừa xây dựng.”
- “Mục tiêu khởi đầu của bạn.”
- “Lưu kế hoạch.”
- “Mở khóa cách thực hiện kế hoạch.”

Do not imply that the user owns a paid service before payment. The free result is theirs; the ongoing Nutree system requires activation.

### 3.8 Social proof

Use only verified, current, locally relevant proof.

Preferred:

- Vietnam App Store rating.
- Number of verified ratings.
- Short sourced review excerpts.
- Real product screenshots.

Avoid:

- Unsourced user counts.
- Anonymous “before/after” claims.
- Testimonials that promise guaranteed weight loss.
- Ratings rendered without platform/date context.

### 3.9 Loss aversion, used carefully

The email screen may say:

> Lưu kế hoạch để bạn không phải nhập lại thông tin.

This is a real continuity benefit.

Do not say:

- “Đừng đánh mất cơ hội.”
- “Kết quả sẽ biến mất sau 10 phút.”
- “Bạn sẽ thất bại nếu rời đi.”

No artificial expiration.

## 4. Stage-by-stage psychology map

| Stage | User action | Psychological function | Required reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing | Chooses to begin | Sets expectations and informed intent | Clear outcome, time, free initial result, eventual price |
| Goal | Names desired change | Self-definition | Goal-specific microcopy |
| Name | Provides first name | Self-reference | Used later, not immediately spammed |
| Challenges | Admits real friction | Problem recognition | Later product emphasis changes |
| Duration | States history | Narrative investment | Reflection tone changes |
| Motivation | Defines positive outcome | Future orientation | Reflection includes it |
| Reflection | Reads summary | Perceived listening | Specific, non-generic synthesis |
| Body basics | Gives age/sex | Accuracy investment | Explain why needed |
| Body metrics | Gives height/weight | Effort and credibility | Visible validated values |
| Target weight | Defines direction | Goal specificity | Optional no-number path |
| Body review | Confirms data | Explicit commitment | Editability and trust |
| Routine | Describes activity/training | Procedural personalization | Result cites routine |
| Eating pattern | Identifies hard moment | Product relevance | Demo uses that moment |
| Diet | States constraints | Practical credibility | Example respects constraints |
| Support style | Chooses plan style | Self-authored solution | Tone and benefits adapt |
| Plan summary | Reviews assembled plan | Ownership/endowed progress | Concise checklist |
| Calculation | Waits briefly | Anticipation | Real, answer-derived stages |
| Result | Receives outcome | Reward | Useful numbers and product demonstration |
| Email | Saves work | Micro-commitment/continuity | Clear next payment step |
| Paywall | Activates plan | Consistency and value exchange | Exact price, benefits, proof, terms |
| Success | Opens app | Completion and continuity | No repeated onboarding; first action |

## 5. Copy voice

### 5.1 Brand character

Nutree should sound:

- Calm.
- Specific.
- Competent.
- Practical.
- Non-judgmental.
- Vietnamese-first.
- Clear about uncertainty.
- Encouraging without hype.

Nutree should not sound:

- Like a motivational influencer.
- Like a medical diagnosis.
- Like an AI demo.
- Like translated US direct-response copy.
- Like a luxury SaaS dashboard.
- Like a game of chance.

### 5.2 Sentence style

Prefer:

- Short sentences.
- Concrete verbs.
- One idea per paragraph.
- Familiar Vietnamese.
- Exact units and dates.
- “Calo,” “protein,” “carb,” and “chất béo” consistently.

Avoid:

- “Tối ưu hóa hành trình sức khỏe toàn diện.”
- “Khai phá phiên bản tốt nhất của bạn.”
- “Được hỗ trợ bởi AI tiên tiến.”
- “Dựa trên khoa học” repeated without explaining the actual method.
- Excessive exclamation marks.
- English labels when a clear Vietnamese equivalent exists.

### 5.3 Headline formula

A good headline should do one of three jobs:

1. Ask one clear question.
2. Confirm one concrete milestone.
3. State one specific value.

Examples:

- “Điều gì thường làm kế hoạch của bạn bị gián đoạn?”
- “Nutree đã có đủ dữ liệu để tính mục tiêu khởi đầu.”
- “Mục tiêu khởi đầu của bạn: 1.642 calo mỗi ngày.”

Avoid combining question, promise, proof, and urgency in one headline.

## 6. Personalization rules

### 6.1 Personalization must be earned

A sentence is personalized only when it uses an answer or calculated output.

Allowed:

> Vì bạn thường khó kiểm soát vào buổi tối, Nutree sẽ giúp bạn nhìn rõ phần calo còn lại trước bữa tối.

Not allowed:

> Kế hoạch này được cá nhân hóa 100% cho bạn.

### 6.2 Avoid false causal certainty

Good:

> Mục tiêu được ước tính từ tuổi, chiều cao, cân nặng, mức vận động và mục tiêu bạn chọn.

Bad:

> Đây là chính xác những gì cơ thể bạn cần.

Use “ước tính,” “mục tiêu khởi đầu,” and “có thể điều chỉnh.”

### 6.3 Challenge templates

#### No time

> Kế hoạch sẽ ưu tiên cách ghi nhận nhanh và những lựa chọn bữa ăn dễ thực hiện.

#### Do not know what to eat

> Kế hoạch sẽ ưu tiên gợi ý bữa ăn cụ thể thay vì chỉ đưa ra một con số calo.

#### Cravings/snacking

> Kế hoạch sẽ giúp bạn nhìn rõ phần còn lại trong ngày và dành chỗ cho bữa phụ.

#### Inconsistent routine

> Kế hoạch sẽ tập trung vào nhịp theo dõi có thể duy trì, không yêu cầu ngày nào cũng hoàn hảo.

#### Eating out

> Kế hoạch sẽ ưu tiên tính linh hoạt và cách cân bằng lại ngân sách dinh dưỡng trong tuần.

#### Conflicting advice

> Kế hoạch sẽ gom thông tin thành một mục tiêu rõ ràng để bạn không phải tự ghép nhiều nguồn.

#### Stress eating

> Kế hoạch sẽ dùng cách theo dõi không phán xét và giúp bạn quay lại nhịp bình thường sau một ngày khó.

Do not imply treatment for emotional eating or mental-health conditions.

### 6.4 Duration changes tone

| Duration | Tone |
|---|---|
| Just starting | Simple, educational, low pressure |
| A few months | Practical correction and structure |
| Around a year | Acknowledge effort; focus on a more sustainable system |
| Several years | Avoid promises; emphasize clarity, flexibility, and support |

### 6.5 Motivation changes emotional payoff

| Motivation | Copy emphasis |
|---|---|
| Confidence | Feeling more comfortable and in control |
| Energy | Stable daily routine and enough nutrition |
| Health | Clear, sustainable habits; no medical promise |
| Clothes | Practical body-goal context without shame |
| Training | Fuel, protein, and recovery |
| Clarity | One understandable target and next action |

### 6.6 Support style changes interface emphasis

#### Simple

Show:

- Main calorie target.
- Protein target.
- One next action.
- Fewer secondary metrics.

#### Flexible

Show:

- Weekly budget.
- Eating-out flow.
- Recovery after exceeding a day.

#### Detailed

Show:

- Macro breakdown.
- Trend view.
- Input explanation.

#### Gentle

Show:

- Sustainable language.
- No-perfect-day framing.
- Easy return after missed tracking.

The underlying nutrition guardrails do not change based on style.

## 7. Progress and timing

### Progress UI

Use:

- Chapter name.
- Step within chapter.
- Completed chapters.

Example:

```text
Cơ thể của bạn
Bước 2/4
```

Do not display “Question 11/24” if it makes the experience feel bureaucratic.

### Auto-advance

Auto-advance only when:

- There is one unambiguous choice.
- The selection is easy to reverse with Back.
- No additional explanation is necessary.

Do not auto-advance:

- Multi-select.
- Body measurements.
- Summary confirmation.
- Paywall.
- Legal consent.

### Calculation timing

- Start real work immediately.
- Keep visible for approximately 0.6–1.2 seconds minimum.
- Never use a fixed four-second delay.
- If processing takes longer, update honest stages.
- Do not use fake percentages.

## 8. Price and payment psychology

The user must know before email submission that payment comes next.

### Landing disclosure

Use secondary copy:

> Kế hoạch đầy đủ trong app: 199.000đ/tháng qua MoMo. Bạn xem mục tiêu khởi đầu trước khi quyết định.

### Email disclosure

Use:

> Bước tiếp theo là gói Nutree Premium 199.000đ/tháng qua MoMo. Gói web này không có dùng thử miễn phí.

### Paywall

Frame payment as activation:

> Mở khóa kế hoạch [name] vừa xây dựng

But display the transaction plainly:

- 199.000đ charged today.
- Automatic monthly renewal.
- Exact next billing date.
- How to manage/cancel.
- No trial.

Do not rely on sunk cost to overcome hidden terms. Psychological investment should increase confidence, not weaken informed consent.

---

## 9. Dark-pattern prohibition

The following are banned:

- Fake countdown timers.
- Fake stock or slot scarcity.
- “Only today” without a real expiring promotion.
- Spinning discount wheel.
- Random-looking coupon that everyone receives.
- Hidden cheaper plan.
- Preselected annual charge.
- Monthly-equivalent price without total.
- Tiny renewal copy.
- Disabled Back button.
- Repeated decline confirmations.
- Body-shaming copy.
- “You are unhealthy” labels used as pressure.
- Fear-based health claims.
- Misleading before/after visuals.
- Fabricated progress trajectory.
- “AI analyzed millions of data points” without substantiation.
- Default body values accepted without action.
- Email capture that conceals the next paywall.
- Purchase event fired before server confirmation.

---

## 10. Copy review checklist

Before release, every screen must pass:

- Does the headline have one clear job?
- Does the user know why this information is requested?
- Does the answer change something later?
- Is the copy specific to Nutree’s real product?
- Is there any generic “AI-powered” language?
- Is there any unsupported promise?
- Is the user being shamed or frightened?
- Is price or renewal being obscured?
- Is the name used naturally?
- Can the user go back and edit?
- Does the screen provide a reward after meaningful effort?
- Would the sentence still make sense if shown to any user? If yes, it may not be truly personalized.
- Is the Vietnamese natural, direct, and locally understandable?
